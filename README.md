 Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

Objective

To perform a thorough Exploratory Data Analysis on a retail sales
dataset to uncover sales patterns, customer behaviour trends,
product performance and actionable business insights.

Technology Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

1. Initial Data Inspection
The dataset is inspected to understand its structure, dimensions,
data types, missing values and duplicate records.

3. Data Cleaning
The dataset contains some missing categorical values and numerical
values stored as strings. These values are converted into appropriate
data types and missing values are handled before analysis.

 3. Descriptive Statistics
Descriptive statistics are calculated for the numerical variables
using mean, median, mode and standard deviation. These measures help
understand the central tendency and variability of the data.

4. Monthly Sales Trend
Monthly net sales are analyzed to identify seasonal patterns and
changes in sales performance throughout the year.

Observation
October recorded the highest monthly net sales, while May recorded
the lowest. The substantial variation between months indicates
seasonal or periodic changes in customer demand.

5. Quarterly Sales Trend
Quarterly net sales are analyzed to identify broader sales patterns
across the four quarters of 2025.

 Observation
Net sales declined from Q1 to Q2 but increased strongly during Q3
and Q4. Q4 recorded the highest quarterly net sales of approximately
2.05 billion VND, indicating strong year-end demand.

6. Customer Type Analysis
Customer types are analyzed to understand the composition of the
customer bse.

Observation
Individual customers represent the majority of orders with 104 orders,
while corporate clients account for 45 orders. This indicates that
individual customers form the primary customer segment.
Dataset Limitation
The dataset does not contain customer age or gender information.
Therefore, age-group and gender analysis could not be performed.
Customer Type was used as an alternative customer segmentation
variable.
7. Top 10 Best-Selling Products
The top 10 products are identified based on total quantity sold.

Observation
Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra is the best-selling product with 40 units
sold, followed by the LG UltraGear 27 inch Monitor with 36 units.
High-demand products should receive priority in inventory planning
to reduce the risk of stockouts.
8. Revenue by Product Category
Net sales are compared across product categories to identify the
major contributors to overall revenue.
Observation
Laptops & Computers generate the highest revenue, followed by
Smartphones & Accessories. Smart Devices and Home Appliances & Living
contribute considerably less revenue. The company should continue
supporting high-performing categories while evaluating strategies
to improve lower-performing categories.
9. Correlation Analysis
A correlation matrix is used to examine relationships between
numerical variables such as quantity, revenue, cost, discounts and
profit.
Observation
Net Sales and Profit show a very strong positive correlation of 0.96.
Quantity Sold also has a moderate positive relationship with Profit
(0.58). Discount Rate has a weak negative relationship with Profit
(-0.20). These relationships help identify variables associated with
business profitability.
10. Additional Analysis – Profit by Region
Regional profitability is analyzed to identify differences in
business performance across geographical markets.
Observation
The South Region generates the highest total profit, followed by the
North Region, while the Central Region generates the lowest profit.
The company should investigate differences in pricing, product mix,
operating costs and demand across regions.
11. Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritize high-demand products:**  
   Maintain sufficient inventory for top-selling products such as the
   Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra and LG UltraGear Monitor.

2. **Prepare for Q4 demand:**  
   Q4 generated the highest quarterly sales. Inventory and marketing
   resources should be increased before the fourth quarter.

3. **Improve weak sales periods:**  
   May recorded the lowest monthly sales. Targeted promotions and
   seasonal campaigns could help improve demand during weaker periods.

4. **Strengthen profitable regions:**  
   The South Region generated the highest profit. Successful strategies
   from this region can be studied and adapted to weaker regions.

5. **Optimize sales channels:**  
   Direct Retail Stores generated the highest sales, while online
   channels also contributed significantly. The company should
   strengthen both physical and digital sales strategies.
12. Conclusion
The Exploratory Data Analysis of the Vietnam Electronics Retail Sales
dataset revealed important patterns in sales, customer types, products,
product categories, regions and sales channels.

Quarterly analysis showed that Q4 was the strongest quarter, while
monthly analysis revealed significant fluctuations in sales throughout
the year.

Product analysis identified Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra as the
best-selling product. Laptops & Computers generated the highest
category revenue, while Direct Retail Stores were the strongest sales
channel.

The correlation analysis showed a strong positive relationship between
Net Sales and Profit. Regional analysis also revealed differences in
profitability, with the South Region generating the highest total
profit.

Based on these findings, the business should focus on high-demand
products, prepare for Q4 demand, improve weaker sales periods,
strengthen profitable regions and optimize sales channels.

The dataset does not contain customer age or gender information, so
those two demographic analyses could not be performed.
